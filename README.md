### 📑 Lessons Curriculum / Darslar Mundarijasi / Содержание уроков

* [**01-introduction-to-kuka-agilus**: Introduction to KUKA KR AGILUS Manipulator & Safety Standards](./01-introduction-to-kuka-agilus)
  * 🇺🇿 KR 6 R900-2 mexanik anatomiyasi, o'qlar joylashuvi (A1..A6), KRC4/KRC5 kontrollerlari va ISO 10218 xalqaro xavfsizlik normalari.
  * 🇷🇺 Механическая анатомия KR 6 R900-2, кинематическая схема осей (A1..A6), контроллеры KRC4/KRC5 и нормы безопасности ISO 10218.
  * 🇬🇧 Mechanical anatomy of KR 6 R900-2, axis layout (A1..A6), KRC4/KRC5 controllers, and ISO 10218 industrial safety standards.

* [**02-smartpad-and-coordinate-systems**: SmartPAD Interface & Mechanics of Coordinate Systems](./02-smartpad-and-coordinate-systems)
  * 🇺🇿 smartPAD boshqaruvi, 6D Space Mouse fizikasi. WORLD, BASE, TOOL va JOINT koordinata tizimlari transformatsiyasi.
  * 🇷🇺 Управление через smartPAD, физика 6D Space Mouse. Трансформация систем координат WORLD, BASE, TOOL и JOINT.
  * 🇬🇧 smartPAD operations, 6D Space Mouse mechanics. Kinematic transformations between WORLD, BASE, TOOL, and JOINT frames.

* [**03-simulation-setup-digital-twin**: Virtual Simulation & Digital Twin Setup (KUKA.Sim Pro & OfficeLite)](./03-simulation-setup-digital-twin)
  * 🇺🇿 KUKA.Sim Pro va KUKA OfficeLite VRC ko'prigi, KR 6 R900-2 uchun raqamli egizak (Digital Twin) muhitini sozlash.
  * 🇷🇺 Мост взаимодействия KUKA.Sim Pro и KUKA OfficeLite VRC, развертывание цифрового двойника для KR 6 R900-2.
  * 🇬🇧 Communication bridge between KUKA.Sim Pro and KUKA OfficeLite VRC, deploying the KR 6 R900-2 digital twin environment.

* [**04-safety-and-safeoperation**: Industrial Safety & KUKA SafeOperation Technology](./04-safety-and-safeoperation)
  * 🇺🇿 ISO 13849-1 va ISO/TS 15066 normalari. SafeOperation monitoring zonalari va xavfsiz tezlik cheklovlari.
  * 🇷🇺 Нормативы ISO 13849-1 и ISO/TS 15066. Зоны мониторинга SafeOperation и ограничения скорости.
  * 🇬🇧 ISO 13849-1 and ISO/TS 15066 standards. SafeOperation monitoring zones, axis limits, and reduced speed monitoring.
